## Description

First Colors is a calm, joyful first coloring book for children ages 2 to 6.

MADE FOR LITTLE HANDS
• Big buttons and big shapes, so even toddlers can tap the right part
• Fill mode: tap a part to color it
• Crayon mode: scribble freely; the crayon stays inside the part where it started
• Eraser and Undo, so nothing is ever a mistake

GENTLE COLOR HELP
• Matching colors glow (red, green or yellow for an apple), but any color is allowed
• Hint shows a soft preview of real colors
• Magic colors the whole picture in one tap

20 FRIENDLY PICTURES
• Fruits: apple, banana, orange, strawberry, grapes, watermelon, pear, cherry, pineapple, mango
• Veggies: carrot, tomato, broccoli, eggplant, corn, potato, cucumber, pumpkin, onion, peas

CELEBRATE AND KEEP
• Confetti and stars when a picture is done
• My Art keeps every finished picture, and you can reopen it to keep coloring
• Up to four children, each with their own art and a cute avatar (no names needed)

FOR GROWN-UPS (behind a press-and-hold lock)
• Sound, music and voice language settings
• Save pictures to Photos
• Back up all art to a file and restore it later
• See storage used and clear art

SAFE AND PRIVATE
• Works fully offline. Nothing leaves the device.
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no tracking, no data collected.

## App Store Connect settings

- Primary category: **Kids**, age band **5 and Under**. Secondary: Education.
- Age rating: 4+.
- App Privacy: **Data Not Collected**.
- Privacy policy URL: **required for Kids apps.** Host a short page stating that the app collects
  no data and works offline (see `docs/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`).
- Export compliance: no encryption (`ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO` is already set).
